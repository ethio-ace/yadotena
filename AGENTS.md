## Layout & routing

- `(dashboard)/layout.tsx` owns shared chrome (sidebar + header + `p-3 sm:p-4 md:p-6` padding). Shell routes (`/dashboard/manager`, `/dashboard/owner`, `/dashboard/kitchen`) render their own full-screen chrome and the layout skips padding for them — **never** add page-level `p-4 md:p-8` wrappers to shared-chrome pages or you double the padding (this was the "view is very large" bug on the orders page).
- The one source of truth for role nav is `src/lib/nav.ts` (`managerNavGroups`, `ownerNavGroups`, `waiterNavGroups`). Shell sidebars and the shared `Sidebar` both render from it; sidebar collapse is persisted via `hooks/useSidebarCollapse.ts` (localStorage).

## Customer (public) flow

- Customers are **anonymous** (no login/register; the customer header has no staff-portal link) but **can order for their table — a waiter approves every guest order** (owner's decision 2026-11-19, reversing the earlier view-only rule). Guest `POST /orders` is AllowAny + throttled: the serializer forces DINE_IN, requires a real `tableId`, strips phone/address, and `create_order(guest=True)` stamps items **`DRAFT`** (order status DRAFT for a new ticket). A guest's second order merges into the table's open ticket as a new DRAFT round. `add-items` stays staff-only. `ItemDetailModal` lets guests pick add-ons (sent as **ids** — the server ignores names), an optional spice level, and a note.
- Public order tracking: `GET /api/v1/orders/lookup?number=` resolves a full order id or the 6-char ticket suffix (see `lookupOrderByNumber`). The header "Track Order" popover and the order-not-found page push to `/order/{number}`; the page itself resolves via `api.orders.lookup` first (id or ticket) and falls back to `getById`. The `/order/[id]` page never settles bills directly — it mirrors the staff `OrderDetailsModal` (shared `OrderProgressStepper`, item cards with images/addons, financial breakdown, payment records) and only shows read-only payment methods/accounts (`PaymentMethodsModal`, powered by the public `GET /payment-methods`) plus `WAITER`/`BILL` service requests. Call-waiter actions are gated to `DINE_IN && status not COMPLETED/CANCELLED`; the modal's "Call Waiter to Settle Bill" only renders for those orders (takeaway/delivery get counter-pay copy).
- "Top Products": `GET /api/v1/menu/popular` (public, no auth) ranks items by total ordered quantity (`orderCount`); the menu/shop pages render it via `TopProductsRow` and fall back to `Popular`/`Favorite` dietary tags or first items when order history is empty. `SortSelect` + `sortCatalogItems` provide popularity/name/price sorting on both pages.
- Guest cart lives in `CustomerDineInContext` + `CustomerDineInCart` (not `useCartStore`, which is the staff POS). Adding a dish must **never** auto-open the cart or the table picker (that was the "review after one item" complaint) — the bottom bar opens it. The cart shows items total only (VAT/service are added on the bill). Guests can't list orders, so the context tracks the order this device placed (`rememberOrder`, localStorage per table) via public `GET /orders/{id}`. The `/menu` page is one scrolling list. Phones: a 56px sticky bar under the fixed `h-16` header (`top-[64px]`) — all-categories sheet button, scroll-spied chips (active = `primary`), search icon that swaps the chips for a field. Desktop (`lg`): sticky category rail with search + counts beside a 2-column card grid. "Popular here" is a swipeable card strip (not repeated rows). Rows are text-first with the photo on the right and the `+`/stepper on the photo; `DishImage` draws the dish initial under the photo so slow/failed images never leave a blank tile. Section scroll offset is `STICKY_OFFSET` (128px) — keep it in sync with the bar height. `scrollbar-none` is a custom `@utility` in globals.css.

## Kitchen (KDS) & order state model

- **Rounds are the kitchen unit of work, not orders.** Every `order_items` row has a `round_number` (1 = original, 2+ = appended later via add-items or dine-in auto-merge) **and its own kitchen `status`** (PENDING → PREPARING → READY → SERVED / CANCELLED) plus `started_at`/`completed_at` (migration 000016). One order can hold several rounds in different states at once — round 1 PREPARING while round 2 just arrived PENDING — and the KDS shows each round as its own card in its own column.
- **The order's kitchen status is derived, never hand-set.** `recomputeOrderKitchenStatusTx` (backend) and `deriveOrderStatus`/`roundStatus` (frontend `lib/kitchen.ts`) use the same rule: READY wins over PREPARING over PENDING over DRAFT; SERVED only when every item is served. Do **not** add back a `SET status='PENDING'` on append — appending must never re-open started work (that was the pre-000016 bug).
- **Kitchen transitions go through `POST /orders/{id}/kitchen`** with `{ roundNumber?, itemIds?, action: start|ready|serve|cancel }` — round-scoped and concurrency-safe (`FOR UPDATE`). The legacy `PATCH /orders/{id}/status` still works for other callers (manager boards, dispatch pane): PREPARING/READY/SERVED are translated to item-level transitions, COMPLETED requires payment PAID + no PENDING/PREPARING items and auto-serves READY items (handover), CANCELLED is terminal. **Kitchen actions are gated per-round, never by whole-order status**: only CANCELLED orders are hard-blocked (`/kitchen` 409) — a COMPLETED order is still cookable if it carries a fresh round, and the per-round status conditions in the UPDATE are the real guard. A settled (PAID) order whose last kitchen work is served auto-completes on the SERVED transition (frees the table), so tickets never linger as SERVED + PAID.
- **Ticket closing is centralised in `_settle_lifecycle` (orders/services.py)** and runs after every kitchen action and whole-order serve: all lines cancelled → CANCELLED; PAID + every live line SERVED → COMPLETED. Kitchen CANCEL (not just guest REJECT) recalculates totals. `_sync_table_for_order` on a terminal ticket frees the table **and** closes its active dining session.
- **Paid-then-extended tickets:** appending a round to a PAID ticket flips `payment_status` back to PENDING. Orders expose `amountPaid` / `balanceDue`; `record_payment` always records exactly `balanceDue` (client `amount` is only validated), so a second settlement charges just the new rounds. `PaymentSettlementModal` and the order-detail views show the balance.
- **Counter/shop sales:** a staff `POST /orders` with `status: COMPLETED|SERVED` creates items SERVED (never on the KDS) and the ticket closes on payment. Guests can't use this. Line quantity is capped at 99 (`MAX_ITEM_QUANTITY`).
- **Frontend round model:** `buildRoundCards(orders)` in `lib/kitchen.ts` produces one card per round; `KitchenBoard`/`KitchenColumn`/`KitchenOrderCard` render them; `OrderDetailSheet` shows per-round status + per-round action buttons; the waiter `TableDetailView` groups items by round with "Added later · Round N" chips; `OrdersBoard` READY tab and "Serve Ready (N)" use item statuses. Legacy orders without per-item status fall back to the whole-order status (`hasItemStatuses` guard) so the UI stays correct before/after the backend deploys.
- **Colour system "Shemma & Tibeb" (`src/app/globals.css`)** — one job per hue: **shewa green** = brand, primary actions, ready, paid (`bg-primary`); **kibbeh gold** = new / needs approval / pending / alerts; **berbere red** = late, unpaid, destructive; **ergo blue** = informational, incl. *cooking/preparing*; **jacaranda violet** = delivery; **roast** = all neutrals (dark mode is coffee-roast, not navy). Tailwind families are **aliased** in `@theme` (emerald/green/teal→shewa, amber/orange/yellow→kibbeh, rose/red→berbere, blue/sky/indigo→ergo, purple/violet→jacaranda, zinc/slate/gray/neutral/stone→roast), so `amber-*` *means* attention — never use it for a CTA or nav selection (use `primary`), and never use `primary` for an alert (use amber). Ramps are generated + contrast-checked (OKLCH); text on `amber-500` is `text-amber-950`. Chart hexes: revenue `#006746`, expenses `#da5042`, net `#e6a313`, previous `#a9a39e`. The `.tibeb` woven band is the only ornament: customer header edge, login card, receipts — not inside working screens.
- KDS stays calm on the roast-dark base: gold only for NEW (column, timers, START PREPARING), neutral for PREPARING, green for READY, berbere only for URGENT borders/labels.

## Staff operations

- Waiter workspace (`/dashboard/waiter`) is **orders-only by design** (per the owner): the layout hides the shared Header/Sidebar for WAITER and redirects every other route (incl. `/dashboard/notifications`) back to it. `WaiterSidebar` owns all chrome — New Order / Shop Sale, three tabs (Tables · Orders · Calls), sound toggle, sign-out; on mobile a bottom tab bar with a centre "+" that hides in focus screens (order entry, table detail). Don't add metrics, search, or links to other system pages here.
- The flow: **Tables** (home, `TablesView`) → free table opens `CafeOrderBuilder` straight away; busy table opens `TableDetailView` (rounds + per-item status, actions: Add items / Mark N served / Settle bill / Ticket). "Add items" re-enters the same `CafeOrderBuilder`, which switches to append mode when its target table has a live order — there is only ONE menu/cart UI (the old `TableAddItemsPanel` and `WaiterHome` are gone). After send/append the page toasts and returns to Tables. The cafe cart is cleared whenever the builder opens for a different table so items never leak between tables.
- `CafeOrderBuilder`: step 1 "where to?" (Takeaway or a table; busy tables say "Add to #…") unless a table was preselected; step 2 tap a card = add one, card footer "Add-ons"/"Note" opens the options sheet. Desktop (`lg`) keeps the cart in a right rail; smaller screens use a bottom "Review order" bar → drawer.
- `OrdersBoard` tabs are Ready to serve (oldest first) / Active / Unpaid / Done today (capped at 30); `ordersForTab` is exported so the page badges the Ready count. Progress everywhere comes from `orderProgress` in `lib/kitchen.ts` via `OrderProgressLine` ("1 ready · 2 cooking").
- Waiter approval of guest orders: `POST /orders/{id}/kitchen` with `approve` (DRAFT→PENDING, enters the KDS queue) or `reject` (DRAFT→CANCELLED; a ticket left with only cancelled items becomes CANCELLED and frees the table). Cancelled lines are excluded from totals. COMPLETED is refused while DRAFT items exist, and payment no longer auto-completes a ticket with DRAFT/cooking work. Waiter UI: orange "N guest orders to approve" chip + "New" badge on the floor, guest round with Reject/Approve in `TableDetailView`, "Guest orders" tab in `OrdersBoard`, chime + toast on arrival. Kitchen surfaces never show DRAFT (`activeRoundCards`, BatchView filters to PENDING/PREPARING). `OrderDetailsModal`'s legacy whole-order review banner stays opt-in (nobody passes the handlers).
- **`isOrderActive` (lib/tableUtils.ts) = not COMPLETED/CANCELLED, PAID or not** — mirrors the backend's `OPEN_ORDER_STATUSES`. Treating PAID as closed made tables that paid early look free while their food cooked, and new orders merged into the paid ticket unseen.
- **Table busy-state is always derived from the live active order, never the stored `table.status`** (stale statuses like a leftover `OCCUPIED` on a free table showed "Occupied" with no order — Table 09 bug). Calls (`AlertsView`) and the floor resolve table names via `useTableLabels`; a table with a pending call gets a red ring on the floor.
- The shared `Header` search is desktop-only (`hidden md:block`) but functional: live dropdown across orders (ticket/customer/table), tables, and dishes. The waiter page still seeds its view from `?tab=orders|tables|calls` via a lazy `useState` initializer.
- Notifications: `NotificationsView` (components/notifications) is the single source for pending + resolved service requests with status/type filters and a per-item detail panel; it powers the shared `/dashboard/notifications` route (manager/owner; waiters use their own Calls tab). The shared `Header` bell dropdown resolves table names via `useTableLabels` (the backend service-request payload has no `tableName`), closes on outside click, and links to "View All".
- Service-request `notes` from the customer order page now carry the human table label; BILL notes for cash omit the amount by design ("is about to pay with Cash at Table X"), digital notes name method + table.
- Manager/owner Orders page (`/dashboard/orders`) applies page-level filters (order type / table / payment status) via `OrdersFilterBar` and passes `ordersOverride` into `ActiveOrdersTab` / `OrderHistoryTab` (they skip their own query when the prop is present). `OrderHistoryTab` is recent-first and splits closed-but-unpaid tickets into a red "Closed but Unpaid" section so they never masquerade as settled history.

## Money controls (added 2026-11-22)

- **Only payment records move money.** `paymentStatus` is read-only on orders (create ignores it; edits can only change type/customer/notes) and is *derived* by `sync_payment_status` from Payment rows. A refund is its own `Payment` row with status `REFUNDED` and a positive amount; `amount_paid` = PAID − REFUNDED. `POST /payments` always records PAID (no `status` param). Orders can't be deleted (405) — cancel with a reason.
- **Cancel / void / refund need a reason and land in the day-close report.** `POST /orders/{id}/cancel {reason}`: floor staff only for tickets with nothing started (PREPARING/READY/SERVED) and nothing paid; otherwise manager, and any money held is auto-refunded. Kitchen `void` (manager, reason) takes PENDING→SERVED lines off the bill; whenever a bill drops below what was paid (void, kitchen cancel, cancel) `_refund_overpayment` records the excess as a refund by the acting user. `POST /orders/{id}/refund {amount, reason}` is manager-only. UI: `OrderControls` inside `OrderDetailsModal` (waiter "Ticket" view and the manager Orders page, where tickets now open on click).
- **Daily ticket numbers:** `Order.business_date` + `daily_number` (unique together), assigned at create. `orderTicketNumber()` shows `#27` on staff screens; **customer screens keep the 6-char id suffix** because public lookup uses it. Staff search accepts `#27` (today).
- `Order.created_by` = staff who took it (guest orders: the waiter who approved). `POST /orders/{id}/move {tableId}` moves a dine-in ticket; a busy target absorbs it (rounds renumbered after the target's, payments follow) and the source closes CANCELLED with `merged_into` set — day-close excludes merged tickets from cancellations.
- **The café is not VAT-registered** (owner, 2026-11-22): `vat_percent` is 0 (data migration) and VAT lines only render when tax > 0. Delivery fee lives in settings (`delivery_fee`), not code.
- Guest QR key: the table QR link is `/menu?table=<id>&k=<qr_token>`; the customer context stores the key per table and sends `tableKey`. Enforcement is the owner setting `require_table_qr_key` (default **off** — old printed QR codes have no key). QR images are still drawn by api.qrserver.com, which therefore sees the key.
- Recipe stock: `products.MenuIngredient` (menu item → Product + qty per serving), edited in `MenuItemModal` via `RecipeEditor`. Stock is taken once per line (`OrderItem.stock_deducted`) when the line first reaches PREPARING/READY/SERVED (counter sales at create); voids never give stock back; stock may go negative. Pre-existing lines were backfilled as already deducted.
- **Day close** `GET/POST /reports/day-close` (`core/dayclose.py`, page `/dashboard/day-close`): money by payment time (cash basis), by method and by `recorded_by`; expected cash = cash in − cash refunds − cash expenses, where a cash expense is any `payment_method` containing "cash" (the expense form saves "Register Cash Drawer").
- **Revenue everywhere headline = payment records by payment time** (owner `computeOwnerMetrics`, manager today revenue, payments pages), not PAID order totals by `createdAt` — a paid-then-extended ticket flips to PENDING and used to vanish from revenue.

## Owner analytics

- Owner metrics in `lib/owner.ts` bucket payments/orders by client-local day on purpose — the backend `/staff/analytics` date-casts in DB UTC and would misplace early-morning Addis orders. Keep local-instant bucketing consistent across overview + reports.
- Payment records carry inconsistent method strings (`cbe_birr` vs `cbe`); merge through `formatPaymentMethod` in `lib/owner.ts` before counting the payment mix.
- `DrilldownTrend` buckets YEAR/MONTH/WEEK up to the last bucket's **end-of-day** (23:59:59), not midnight, or final-day orders silently drop out of the chart.
- Analytics Hub tabs live in `src/components/owner/reports/`; the reports page itself is a thin tabbed shell. Staff report derives activity from `api.activityLogs.getAll` (limit 500) merged with `/users`.
- Orders carry `tableId` codes (`tbl-04`, legacy `t1`) but **no `tableName`** — display names come only from the `/tables` roster via `hooks/useTableLabels.ts`. Resolve ids lazily; if a component memoizes a customer/key map, the table labels must be in the memo deps or raw fallbacks ("Table bl-04") persist forever (this bit us in the drill dropdown). `useTableLabels` memoizes its map so the identity is stable across renders.
- Order item `selectedAddons` are raw string ids (`addon-gl-01`, UUIDs) with **no names attached** — resolve them through the `['addons']` query map with the `addonNames(addons, map)` helper in `lib/kitchen.ts` (tolerant of string[] and object[] shapes). Every ticket surface that renders addons without the map leaks raw ids (chef BatchView and dashboard KitchenOrderCard both had this bug).

## Auth & data quirks

- **API tokens are one-per-account and shared across devices** (`authentication/tokens.py`). Logging out deletes that token, which signs out *every* device on the account (e.g. the wall KDS when a chef logs out on a tablet). Give shared screens their own accounts until per-device tokens exist.
- Dates that represent a café day use **`timezone.localdate`** / the browser's local `YYYY-MM-DD` (expenses), never UTC `now()`/`toISOString()` — Addis is UTC+3, so late-night entries otherwise land on the previous day.
- Settings accept `PUT` and `PATCH` (both partial). Upload validation treats any Pillow decode error (incl. `SyntaxError` on corrupt PNGs) as a 400. Review ratings are 1–5.
