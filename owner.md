# Owner Analytics & Control Center — Technical & UI Analysis

## 1. Overview & Architectural Purpose
The Owner Control Center (`/dashboard/owner` and sub-routes) provides executive-level financial oversight, revenue analytics, operational performance metrics, and system configuration tools for business owners at **Yadotena Milk & Foods**.
Key Capabilities:
- **Executive Revenue & Profit Analytics**: Real-time tracking of Gross Revenue, Net Income (Revenue minus Expenses), Average Order Value, and channel mix.
- **Dynamic Period Comparison & Drilldown**: Historical trend analysis across customizable date ranges (`Today`, `Yesterday`, `This Week`, `This Month`, `YTD`, `Custom`).
- **Staff Performance & Audit Reporting**: Comprehensive breakdown of employee sales volume, waiter ticket handling, and system activity logs.
- **Payment Infrastructure Management**: Configuring customer-facing digital payment methods (CBE Birr, Telebirr, Bank of Abyssinia, Cash) and account details.

## 2. Component & Sub-Route Breakdown

### 2.1 Main Owner Dashboard (`/dashboard/owner`)
- **Executive Chrome (`OwnerSidebar.tsx` & `OwnerHeader.tsx`)**: Dark-themed executive sidebar (`ownerNavGroups`), date range display badge, attention counter, and mobile menu toggle.
- **Date Range Selector (`DateRangeSelector.tsx`)**: Preset pill picker (`TODAY`, `YESTERDAY`, `THIS_WEEK`, `THIS_MONTH`, `YTD`, `CUSTOM`) supporting custom date range pickers (`CustomRange`).
- **Period Comparison Bar (`PeriodCompare.tsx`)**: Displays percentage growth/decline compared to the preceding period for Revenue, Order Volume, and Average Ticket.
- **Executive Attention Center (`AttentionCenter.tsx`)**: Flags critical financial anomalies (high volume of unpaid open tickets, unrecorded expenses, out-of-stock items).

### 2.2 Primary KPI Cards & Metrics Summary
- **Total Revenue Card**: Prominent ETB total with background ambient glow, order count, and growth trend indicator vs yesterday.
- **Paid Orders Card**: Total cleared transactions and average ticket value (`metrics.averageTicket`).
- **Average Order Value Card**: Transaction size benchmarking against business target (e.g. 500 ETB).
- **Net Income & Expense Breakdown**:
  - `Net Income` = Gross Revenue minus Recorded Expenses.
  - `Recorded Expenses` = Aggregated operational costs.
  - `Order Channels` = Revenue split between `DINE_IN`, `TAKEAWAY`, and `DELIVERY`.

### 2.3 Analytics Charts & Visualizations
- **Revenue Trend Chart (`DrilldownTrend.tsx`)**: Code-split interactive chart displaying daily/hourly revenue trends vs expenses. Supports dynamic time bucketing (`HOUR`, `DAY`, `WEEK`, `MONTH`).
- **Payment Mix Distribution (`PaymentMix.tsx`)**: Visual payment share chart (CBE Birr, Telebirr, BOA, Cash).
- **Hourly Sales Profile (`HourlyProfile.tsx`)**: Hour-by-hour revenue distribution identifying peak operating hours (`peakHour`, `peakRevenue`).
- **Top Selling Products (`TopProducts.tsx`)**: Performance matrix ranking top menu items and retail store products by revenue and quantity sold.
- **Recent Activity Stream (`TodayActivity.tsx`)**: Real-time log of staff operations and system events.

### 2.4 Analytics Hub & Reports (`/dashboard/reports`)
Tabbed reporting interface (`ReportsTabs.tsx`):
- `Staff Productivity` (`StaffReport.tsx`): Analyzes waiter and cashier performance (orders handled, revenue collected per staff member, average order processing time).
- `Expense Breakdown` (`ExpensesReport.tsx`): Detailed categorization of operational costs (raw ingredients, utilities, payroll, repairs).
- `Category Revenue` (`MenuCategoriesReport.tsx`): Contribution of each menu category to overall gross revenue.
- `Product Popularity` (`PopularityReport.tsx`): Matrix ranking item sales volume, re-order rate, and profit contribution.
- `Add-on Performance` (`AddonsReport.tsx`): Revenue generated by dish customizations and extra toppings.
- `Customer Channel Breakdown` (`CustomersReport.tsx`): Dine-in vs Takeaway vs Delivery volume distribution.

### 2.5 Staff & Employee Management (`/dashboard/employees`)
- Roster of store personnel displaying name, email, assigned role (`WAITER`, `CHEF`, `MANAGER`, `OWNER`), and account status.
- Modal to create new staff accounts, update roles, or reset login credentials.

### 2.6 Payment Methods Manager (`/dashboard/payment-methods`)
- Centralized manager (`PaymentMethodsManager.tsx`) for configuring digital bank accounts displayed to customers on `/order/[id]` and staff POS modals.
- Fields: Bank name, account number/IBAN, account holder name, QR code image upload, active toggle switch.

### 2.7 Store Settings (`/dashboard/settings`)
- Configuration page for store profile, receipt footer note, default tax rate (VAT 15%), service charge rate (10%), and currency formatting.

## 3. Data Flow & Metric Calculation Engine
- **Analytics Engine (`lib/owner.ts`)**: Centralized analytics library that processes raw orders, items, and expenses:
  - `computeOwnerMetrics()`: Calculates revenue, paid order count, unpaid totals, average ticket, net income, payment mix, and channel distributions.
  - **Local-Instant Bucketing**: Buckets orders by client-local day/time to prevent time-zone shifts from misclassifying late-night Addis Ababa orders.
- **Custom Hook (`useOwnerOps.ts`)**: Manages query fetching (`orders`, `expenses`, `activityLogs`, `users`) and re-calculates metrics dynamically when `rangeKey` changes.

## 4. Strengths & Implemented UI Highlights
- **Executive Elegance**: High-end dark theme design with curated color palettes, glassmorphism cards, ambient gradient glows, and crisp typography.
- **Robust Payment Mix Merging**: Normalizes inconsistent payment method strings (`cbe_birr` vs `cbe`) using `formatPaymentMethod` to ensure clean financial charts.
- **Code-Split Charting**: Chart components (`DrilldownTrend`, `PaymentMix`, `HourlyProfile`) are lazily loaded via `next/dynamic` to keep initial bundle size minimal.

## 5. Audit: Issues, Edge Cases & Things That Need Fix

### 5.1 Staff Performance Sample Limit (Data Truncation Bug)
- **Issue**: In `StaffReport.tsx`, staff activity data is derived by fetching activity logs with `limit: 500` (`api.activityLogs.getAll({ limit: 500 })`). In a busy restaurant generating thousands of events per week, long-range staff reports only sample the 500 most recent actions, under-reporting waiter totals for older dates.
- **Fix**: Query staff revenue directly from the `/staff/analytics` backend endpoint or increase audit log fetch pagination.

### 5.2 Destructive Payment Account Deletion (UX Safety Risk)
- **Issue**: In `PaymentMethodsManager.tsx`, clicking the delete icon next to a bank payment method immediately removes the payment account without displaying a confirmation modal.
- **Fix**: Wrap delete actions in a confirmation dialog ("Are you sure you want to delete this payment account?").

### 5.3 Net Income Misrepresentation on Unrecorded Expenses (Financial Report Bug)
- **Issue**: `metrics.revenueMinusExpenses` computes Gross Revenue minus Manual Expenses. If the store owner has not manually logged inventory expenses for a given week, Net Income displays equal to Gross Revenue, which could give an inaccurate profit impression if unannotated.
- **Fix**: Add a small note badge near Net Income: *"Based on recorded manual expenses. Add inventory costs in Expenses for full P&L."*

### 5.4 Chart Axis Label Overlap on Mobile Devices (Responsive Chart Issue)
- **Issue**: In `DrilldownTrend.tsx`, when selecting `YEAR_TO_DATE` or dynamic custom ranges spanning many months, chart X-axis labels condense and overlap on mobile screens (<640px).
- **Fix**: Set dynamic `interval="preserveStartEnd"` or reduce X-axis tick frequency on mobile viewports.
